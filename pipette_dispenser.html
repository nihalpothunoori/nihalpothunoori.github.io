Pipette Dispenser

Overview

Graduate student required additional functionality to complete an autosampler for the Autonomous Materials Systems Group at UIUC.

Technical Details

I spent over 100 hours in Fusion360 over the course of this project. There were two parts: a rudimentary robotic "arm" and an automatic pipette dispenser.

For the robotic arm, I simply modeled a few stepper motors, and designed brackets to build a structure like so:

This allowed us to consider the end-effector in a spherical-coordinate system, where an an end-effector position corresponded to three simple rotations of the stepper motors. This system was mounted on a gantry such that the arm could reach anywhere on the autosampling table easily. The purpose: easily move samples, vials, etc around the crowded table.

The second system, the pipette dispenser, was done by me from scratch. I went through a few iterations before I got a working prototype. A few of the early, unfortunate examples:

The system I settled upon draws pipettes from a hopper, one at a time, and dispenses them vertically. I'm particularly proud of my design on the pipette iterator and the orientation mechanism.

Next came the state estimation. Experimentally finding the LeKiwi's process noise/covariance via encoder data, fused with the covariance and measurements from the AprilTag allowed me a pretty decent position estimate. The ROS2 ekf_localization node did most of the heavy lifting here. I chucked the output and the path into the NAV2 Pure Pursuit controller package, and the whole system came together. P.S.

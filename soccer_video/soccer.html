Soccer Counterfactual Video Generation

Skills: VAE, LoRA, Diffusion Transformers, Flow Matching

Overview

Can generate alternate sports realities with text-prompted video generation. Implemented from scratch after reading the Wan paper (VAE, DiT, etc). Experimented with different architectures such as VAEs and Diffusion U-nets before settling on a Wan-based implementation.

Architecture

The pipeline encodes input video frames into a latent space using a Variational Autoencoder (VAE), then applies a Diffusion Transformer (DiT) to perform flow-matching-based generation guided by a text prompt. LoRA adapters were used to fine-tune the model on soccer footage, allowing targeted domain adaptation without full retraining.

Counterfactual Generation

Given a real clip of a soccer play, the model can generate a plausible alternate version of events based on a text description — for example, changing the outcome of a shot or rewriting a player's movement. The approach separates scene structure (field layout, player positions) from the generative diffusion process, so edits stay grounded in the original scene context.
